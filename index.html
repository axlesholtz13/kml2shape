Output notes
The download is a ZIP containing standard shapefile components (.shp, .shx, .dbf, .prj). Mixed geometry inputs are separated into point, line, and polygon layers. KML coordinates are geographic longitude/latitude; define or verify the CRS in your GIS software before analysis.
